Dendrites

Tree-like extensions that receive electrochemical signals from other neurons and conduct them toward the cell body.

AI → Weighted inputs (x₁w₁, x₂w₂, …)

Cell Body (Soma)

The central hub containing the nucleus. It integrates all incoming signals and determines whether the neuron will fire.

AI → Weighted sum Σ(xᵢwᵢ) + b

Axon

A long projection that carries electrical impulses away from the cell body to other neurons, muscles, or glands.

AI → Activation function σ(z)

Synapse

The junction between two neurons where signal transmission occurs via neurotransmitters across the synaptic cleft.

AI → Output ŷ passed to next layer
Activation Function — Sigmoid
σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e−z)
where z = Σ(xᵢ · wᵢ) + b
Maps any real-valued number into (0, 1), mimicking a neuron's fire/don't-fire decision with a smooth, differentiable curve.
